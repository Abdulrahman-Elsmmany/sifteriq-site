A calmer way to clear your Watch Later.

Sifteriq is an AI-assisted organizer for your own YouTube Watch Later list. It imports the videos you saved, adds their details from YouTube, ranks them against a goal you write, and helps you clean up the rest.

Sifteriq is in private development and is not yet available to the public. There is no public sign-up yet.

How it works

From a long backlog to a short list

Sifteriq works with the videos you choose to import. It reads their public details, never the videos themselves, and it changes nothing on YouTube unless you turn on write access and start one of the actions listed below.

  1. 01

    Import

    Upload your Watch Later export from Google Takeout, paste video links, or connect one YouTube playlist you choose with read-only access.

  2. 02

    Enrich

    Sifteriq looks up each video's public details through the YouTube Data API, such as title, channel, description, tags, duration and thumbnail.

  3. 03

    Triage by your goal

    If you turn it on, an AI model run by a third-party provider, reached through OpenRouter, reads each video's title, channel, duration, publish year, tags and the start of its description next to a goal you write and suggests a lane for each video, such as watch now, this week, reference or skip.

  4. 04

    Clean up

    Review the suggestions, keep notes, build a watch plan and export your list. If you allow it, Sifteriq removes the videos you confirm from the playlist you connected.

Sifteriq's recommendations, priority scores, topics and reasons are generated independently by Sifteriq and are not sourced directly from YouTube's API. They are estimates and can be wrong.

Google access

What Sifteriq asks of Google, and why

You sign in to Sifteriq with an email address and password, not with Google. Connecting a YouTube account is optional: a Takeout file or pasted links work without it.

  • youtube.readonly

    Read-only access

    Used to import the playlist you pick:

    • Read your channel's name, so you can see which account is connected.
    • List your playlists, so you can choose one. Only the chosen playlist is saved.
    • Read the videos in that playlist, and check it for new ones.
  • youtube

    Write access, only if you turn it on

    Used for the same reads, plus only these three actions:

    • Create a private playlist named "Sifteriq Inbox" when you ask for one.
    • Remove videos you select and confirm from the playlist you connected.
    • Undo a removal you just made.

Sifteriq never uploads, rates or comments on videos, never downloads videos, and never changes anything else in your account. You can disconnect in Sifteriq at any time, or remove its access on your Google security settings page.

Your data

Your data, briefly

The Privacy Policy has the details. In short:

  • No ads, no tracking

    Sifteriq does not sell your data, show ads, or use analytics or tracking cookies.

  • Only what triage needs

    If you turn on AI triage, the third-party AI provider receives video details and your goal, never your name, email address or notes.

  • Yours to remove

    Export your library, clear it or delete your account. Clearing your library or deleting your account deletes the data involved within 7 days, except for the records listed in the Privacy Policy. We keep records that show a deletion happened. You can disconnect YouTube at any time; that ends Sifteriq's access and deletes the videos imported through it, normally at once.
