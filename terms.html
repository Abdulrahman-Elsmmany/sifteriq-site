What Sifteriq is

Sifteriq is an AI-assisted organizer for your own YouTube Watch Later list and saved playlists. It imports videos you saved and adds their public details from YouTube. If you turn on AI recommendations, it describes what each video covers and is worth watching for, ranks your videos for a goal you write, and groups saved videos that cover the same thing. It also lets you play your videos in Sifteriq with YouTube's player, plan what to watch, and clean up your list.

Sifteriq is in private development. It may change, pause or stop at any time, and features may be added or removed. We do not promise that it will be available, uninterrupted or free of errors.

Connecting YouTube

Connecting a YouTube account is optional. Connect only accounts that you own or are authorized to manage.

If you turn on write access, Sifteriq can act on your YouTube account, but only after you start an action in Sifteriq:

  • creating a private playlist named "Sifteriq Inbox", or reusing one you already have, when you choose to create it (see the Privacy Policy);
  • removing the videos you select and confirm from the playlist you connected;
  • undoing a removal you made.

Sifteriq may suggest that a video could go, with its reason, but it never removes anything on its own. A removal changes your playlist on YouTube, not only Sifteriq. Undo puts a video back at the end of the playlist and can fail, for example if the video is no longer available. You are responsible for the actions you confirm. You can disconnect YouTube in Settings → Connections at any time, or remove Sifteriq's access on your Google security settings page; the Privacy Policy explains what happens to your data in each case.

AI features

Sifteriq's video summaries (what a video covers and what it is worth watching for), fit labels, rankings, reasons, goal profiles and Groups (which saved videos cover the same thing, the video to start with and what each other video adds) are generated independently by Sifteriq and are not sourced directly from YouTube's API. YouTube does not provide or endorse them.

They are estimates produced with third-party AI models from public video details and the goal you write; Sifteriq never reads inside a video. They can be inaccurate, incomplete or out of date, and we make no guarantee about them. Groups can put videos together that do not cover the same thing, or miss ones that do, so check what each video adds before you set one aside. They are not professional advice. You decide what to watch, keep or remove, so check a suggestion before acting on it, especially before removing videos from YouTube.

The AI features (AI recommendations in the app) are optional and run only if you turn them on and agree to the AI notice Sifteriq shows first, which lists what is sent and to whom. When that notice changes to name a new recipient or a new kind of data, Sifteriq pauses the AI features until you agree to the new notice. You can turn them off at any time in Settings.
